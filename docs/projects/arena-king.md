# Arena King

A tabletop dice-and-card game for 2–4 players that Jake designed in February 2017 for SJSU's CS 108 (game design). Players fight with character cards (HP, ATK, DEF), dice, coins and a store of buffs, one card each per match, and win by claiming the most defeated cards. The page is `public/projects/survivor/arena-king.html`; the card id is `arena-king`. The sources are in `Z:\Google Drive\School\College\CS 108\`.

It shared a card with the same class's other game, [Survivor](survivor.md), until 2026-10-27, and still lives in Survivor's folder; that page says why they were split.

## Preview

`arena-king.html` is a hand-written write-up on the same yellow as Survivor's page, from `BoardGame.docx` and `ArenaKing-Rules.docx` (25 February 2017, the same rules; `BoardGame.docx` adds "withholding buffs" to the pitch).
- **The rules:** what's in the box, setup, the four phases of a round, the damage rule (ATK minus DEF, never below zero) and how to win.
- **The buffs:** a table. In the docs, blue buffs (ATK, DEF) last the match and red ones are used once; the table shows that as a tag. The docs give no prices, so the page only says what the playtest shows: the +1, +3 and +5 buffs cost 1, 2 and 3 copper. On a phone each row becomes a small card.
- **The playtest:** summarized from `arena king 1.docx`, Jake's session report: five matches, which the playtester won 3 cards to 2. The closing line about what the log shows (one heavy hitter took three matches; high defense stalled a match until Poison broke it) is a reading of the log, not something the report says.

**Names.** The playtest report names the other player; the page says "a playtester". The card names are Jake's own and kept as written, "Big Ass Sword" included.
