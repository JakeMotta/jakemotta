SJSU CS 134 · 2017

The Floor is Lava

Final project for my game programming class · November 2017

Blocks rain down, the lava rises and the floor sinks into it. A miner digs blocks loose with his pickaxe, picks them up and stacks them under his feet to stay on top.

It's written in Java straight on top of OpenGL (JOGL). There was no engine, so the sprite loading, animation, camera and collisions are mine too.

  • ←→ step
  • ↑ jump
  • Z mine
  • Space drop a block under you
  • Ctrl + arrow turn

The clip was recorded in 2026 from the original 2017 build, with the music off. A small bot that reads the screen plays the miner.
