Brittney Schultz
Brittney is a young athletic woman who is new to inline racing.

She began racing in 2012 and has become a promising team member for Team SAFE.

Living in southern California she is able to train with other very talented skaters, as well as train in some of the most beautiful areas California has to offer.

Brittney loves skating on the beach front and diet coke.
