EzeeFit Sports
It all started with... New speedskating boots, my first marathon and some really trashed feet!

I wrapped my feet with sport tape and hoped for the best but... my feet sweat, so the tape slipped and I got massive sores and blisters, yikes!

This is not the way I envisioned this sport to go so after trying moleskin and all of the commercial blisterpads with no real relief, I started cutting foam neoprene and elastic and sewed my first version of the Ankle Bootie. After many designs, I came up with the current style. After wearing them to a few group skates, other skaters wanted to know where I got them and then, if I could make some to sell. After three years of making a few for friends and everyone they encountered, I decided to go into production with three thicknesses to suit different fit issues.

My girlfriend, who shows her horse, wanted to try them in her tall equestrian boots. She found the 3mm helped in keeping her heels down.

Then several skaters asked for a rain and wind resistant cover. After a rainy 2004 Athens to Atlanta, Kim Perkins gave them a thumbs up for saving her brand new Bont boots. Barry Publow wore them to his victory in the 2005 US National Marathon Championships.

In 2007 we took our products to ISPO in Munich Germany and quickly became a staple in Germany and Switzerland. We now sell to shops throughout Europe including Russia and the Netherlands as well as Australia, New Zealand, South Africa and Asia.

We introduced an Ultra-thin full foot bootie for watersports but quickly found customers using them for skiing and derby because they also keep your feet warm.

Our most recent addition is the all tan version of the Ultra- thin and 2-mm ankle booties requested by dancers and our new anti-friction toe cover for toe blisters.

Susan and Bob
