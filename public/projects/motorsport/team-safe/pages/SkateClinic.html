Team SAFE Hosts Skate Clinic
       2014 is here and Team SAFE Racing is not wasting any time. Team SAFE hosted a clinic on the beautiful Monterey Peninsula during the weekend of January 3rd, 4th and 5th.

In attendance were several Team SAFE skaters: Rob Motta, Chris Rojo, Linus Harth, John Crysdale, Mark Randle, Team Owner Paul Sargenti and a short appearance by Paul Lomangino. Also in attendance was Brent Leaman, Coach Deb Alpers and four young and very talented skaters: Jacob (20 yrs. Old), Cameron (9 yrs. Old), Malav (8 or 9 yrs. Old) and Grace (8 or 9 yrs. Old). These four younger skaters were so much fun to have with us. They kept the energy high!

We were fortunate to have Deb Alpers coaching. Deb brings a wealth of knowledge and has a fun coaching style. She worked well with all of the skaters and had something to offer every skater at every level.

The main focus of the clinic was “back to basics”. No matter the sport or level at which anyone competes, if you don’t have a solid base you don’t have anything. We worked both on and off skate drills all three days. We also did a fun group skate along the world famous 17 mile drive inside Pebble Beach.

All of the skaters had a great time and took home something they can use to help them have a season this year.
