Ryan McGee
Born:
-7/27/1987

I enjoy all types of inline skating; speed skating, urban skating, aggressive skating, and off road skating.

I am originally from Texas and owe the Texas Flyers credit for teaching me the basics of inline racing and training. I moved to Santa Barbara after college to pursue a MS and PhD at UCSB, and now run my own software development business, which gives me the freedom to travel and skate often. I have spent many Saturdays waking up at 6am to make the 120 mile each-way drive down to Long Beach on my motorcycle to train with the SoCal skaters. I’ve had the pleasure of racing with several Team SAFE members over the last few years and am excited to join the team.

I began racing inline skates in 2008 but I have been skating various forms (fun, urban, aggressive, speed) for about 20 years total.

Favorite race:
-Berlin Marathon! I’ve been fortunate enough to go twice now and plan to go back every year as long as I can. I love this race for few reasons; 6,000 plus skaters and a fast course through the heart of the city and lots of history!

I also enjoy cross country skating skiing, snowboarding, cycling, trail running and tower-running.
