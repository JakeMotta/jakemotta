Survivor

GameMaker Studio 2 platformer for my game design class · March–April 2017 · with a classmate, Elias

An innocent prisoner, locked up for a crime he didn't commit, has to break out of a detention center before he's caught. The prison is wired with everything it takes to keep him in: moving platforms, electric barriers, fake floors, elevators and spikes.

  • The arrow keys run and jump.
  • One life, with room for a little damage.
  • Each level is one screen: get from the left side to the right.
  • Five levels, each a little meaner.
  • One clock for the whole escape. When it runs out, he's back in his cell.
He's mostly stripes.
