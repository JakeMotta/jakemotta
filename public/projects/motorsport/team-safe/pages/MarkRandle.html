Mark Randle

We specialize in Directional Drilling, which is a way to install utility lines without trenching.

I have been married for 31 years (wife -Lauri) we have 4 children. (3 girls & 1 boy - oldest 30 youngest 18)

My favorite skate race would have to be Athens to Atlanta.

I seem to keep busy all season with cycling & skating.

I enjoy playing guitar & bass & singing and also writing & recording songs.
