Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This covers jakemotta.com, Jake Motta's personal site, and “jakemotta”, the small Google sign-in app that keeps track of what Jake listens to on YouTube Music.

Visiting the site

  • There are no accounts, sign-ups, ads, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • The site is served through Cloudflare and a web host, which keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested), as nearly every website's host does.
  • Some parts load things from other services, which receive the usual details of each request under their own privacy policies. Archived projects can embed other sites, and a few still contain their original forms, which send whatever you type to the service behind them. The music sections can show cover art and listening data from services such as ListenBrainz, Spotify and YouTube.

The YouTube Music sync

  • The “jakemotta” Google app exists so this site can show what Jake listens to. Only Jake signs in to it, and it isn't offered to anyone else.
  • With his permission, it reads his own YouTube Music listening history: song titles, artists, albums and roughly when he played them. It reads nothing else from his Google account.
  • It copies those plays to Jake's public ListenBrainz profile, which this site displays. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared anywhere else.
  • Its sign-in is kept only as an encrypted secret in this site's GitHub repository, for the job that runs the sync. Access can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings.
  • Its use of YouTube is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.
