# CS 134 games

Two desktop games Jake wrote in 2017 for SJSU's CS 134 (game programming), in Java on top of OpenGL through JOGL 2.3.2, with no engine: the window, sprite loading (from `.tga` files), animation, camera and collisions are all hand-written. The folder is `public/projects/cs134/`; the card id is `cs134`.

- **The Floor is Lava** (final project, November 2017). A miner on a 10-column grid. Blocks drop from the sky (a red `!` warns where), the lava rises and the floor sinks into it. He digs blocks loose with a pickaxe, picks up what drops and places it under his feet to climb. Controls: ←/→ step one block, ↑ jump, Z (held) mine, Space or ↓ drop a carried block underneath, Ctrl + arrow turn without moving, Enter to retry.
- **The Scott Pilgrim side-scroller** (March 2017). Scott Pilgrim idles, runs in four directions and punches (Z) across a scrolling level, throwing a fireball with each punch. Ramona follows him and jumps when he swings. The window title is "I can't charge my phone and listen to music at the same time".

## Preview

`index.html` is a hand-written page with a clip of each game, side by side on wide screens and stacked on phones. The clips are silent, so unlike [Escaping Confinement](escaping-confinement.md) the page doesn't pause the site's music. They autoplay muted and loop; with `prefers-reduced-motion` they're paused on their poster frame instead.

**The clips.** Both games still run on Java 25 (the installed Temurin JRE). JOGL 2.3.2 from 2015 loads its natives without any `--add-opens`; only `--enable-native-access=ALL-UNNAMED` was passed. They were recorded on 2026-10-26.
- **Capture:** frames were grabbed from the game window with `PrintWindow` (`PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT`) at 15 fps. A plain screen copy of a JOGL window comes out black.
- **Input:** key presses were posted straight to the game window, so none could reach another app.
- **Encoding:** Blender's video sequencer, an image strip, the `Standard` view transform, H.264 at the window's own size.
- `floor-is-lava.mp4`: 640×960, 25 s, 0.5 MB. The music and pop sound were swapped for silence in a scratch copy before running, so nothing played on the speakers.
  - **Who's playing:** the miner is played by a small bot that reads each frame. It dodges falling blocks, mines blocks beside it, picks up the drops, climbs onto blocks and places them. The 25 seconds are the second half of a 60-second run, once blocks had piled up and the lava had risen.
  - **Starting a run:** the game opens on its GAME OVER screen, so a run starts with Enter.
- `scott-pilgrim.mp4`: 800×600, 20.7 s, 0.7 MB. The run, punch and fireball moves follow a fixed key script.
- **Posters:** `floor-is-lava.jpg` and `scott-pilgrim.jpg` are each clip's first frame.

## Left out on purpose

- **The games themselves:** they're Java desktop programs and can't run in a browser. The jars, JOGL (`jogamp-fat.jar`, 9 MB) and the source stay in the archive.
- **The Floor is Lava's music:** `main_music.wav` is 40 MB and its origin isn't recorded. The clips are silent.
- **Sprites:** the roughly 100 sprites and the font images aren't copied; the clips show them in use.
- **Other snapshots and homework:** the earlier Scott Pilgrim snapshots (`cs134_game_perfect_scroll.zip`, `cs134_game_able_to_fire.zip`), `hw2_export.zip` (a sprite-walking homework titled "Magicarp Used Fly"), and the unused Mario sheets in `Final_Project/`.
