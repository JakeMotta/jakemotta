Coloris

Greenfoot top-down RPG · 2014 · unfinished · no sound

The original Java game, about a 6 MB download. It needs a keyboard.

Change Huey's color: 1 white, 2 black, 3 red, 4 green, 5 blue, 6 yellow

Coloris never got past its first map: Huey walks west through the forest, past a cliff and its stairs, changing color as he goes. There are no enemies, goals or sounds yet. The scrolling comes from danpost's SWorld class, shared on greenfoot.org.

This is the original Java game, built in Greenfoot in 2014, running in your browser as an applet. Greenfoot never exported it, so in 2026 its compiled classes were packed into a jar with the Greenfoot runtime. Java runs through CheerpJ by Leaning Technologies, which loads from its own servers when you press Play. On a phone it will load, but you'll need a keyboard to play.
