Survivor & Arena King

Two games I designed for a game design class: a platformer about breaking out of prison, and a dice-and-card duel for the table.

Survivor

GameMaker Studio 2 platformer · March–April 2017 · with a classmate, Elias

An innocent prisoner, locked up for a crime he didn't commit, has to break out of a detention center before he's caught. The prison is wired with everything it takes to keep him in: moving platforms, electric barriers, fake floors, elevators and spikes.

  • The arrow keys run and jump.
  • One life, with room for a little damage.
  • Each level is one screen: get from the left side to the right.
  • Five levels, each a little meaner.
  • One clock for the whole escape. When it runs out, he's back in his cell.
He's mostly stripes.

The finished game didn't make it: the only GameMaker project left is an early test room, so there's nothing to play. This is the plan and the art.

Arena King

Tabletop dice-and-card game · February 2017 · 2–4 players

A game about dueling warriors. Fight with your character cards, buy buffs, and keep the good buffs away from everyone else, but don't spend your money too fast.

In the box

  • 12 character cards, each with HP, ATK and DEF
  • 2 or 3 dice
  • Silver and copper coins (1 silver = 5 copper)
  • A buff sheet, and a log sheet for each player

Setup

Everyone draws 3 character cards at random and starts with 5 silver.

A match

One card from each player fights until only one is left standing. Each round:

  1. Pick a card to fight with, face down.
  2. Roll the dice. In that order, each player may buy one buff.
  3. Reveal the cards, apply the buffs and write everything on your log sheet.
  4. Fight, taking turns in the same order: pick an enemy and attack.

Then go back to step 2 until one card is left.

Fighting

Damage is your ATK minus their DEF. If that's zero or less, they take nothing.

Kill a card and you claim it, plus 1 silver. Claimed cards never fight again; the survivor can, and heals 1 HP for each match it sits out.

Winning

Whoever has claimed the most cards wins.

Buffs

Each buff is a single item in the store: only one player can have it, and it goes back once it's used. One buy per player per round, and no sharing money. Hold on to a buff (anything but ATK or DEF) to spring it later, or just to keep it from someone else. ATK and DEF go on at the start of a round, and once one's been used in a match nobody else gets it that match.

In the playtest, the +1, +3 and +5 buffs cost 1, 2 and 3 copper.

Playtest: me against one playtester, five matches

  1. Steven (6 HP, 2 ATK, 5 DEF) against Another Steven (10 HP, 3 ATK, 4 DEF). I stacked ATK +3 and +5 for a 6-damage hit, and Reflect turned it around: 5 damage back, leaving Steven on 1 HP. An HP +3 kept him going, and he won.
  2. Big Ass Sword (5 HP, 8 ATK, 3 DEF) took Square in two hits.
  3. Not A Square against Guy with A Lot of HP (15 HP). With DEF buffs on both sides, nobody could do damage, so my Poison won it a point at a time over 14 turns of swinging for nothing.
  4. Big Ass Sword came back for the last two: Steven in one hit, then Not A Square in two.

Final count: the playtester 3 cards, me 2. One heavy hitter decided three of the five matches, and high defense could stall a fight until something like Poison broke it.
