Natassia Hamor
Where were you born and where do you live now?
- I was born in Santa Clara, California and live in Milpitas, California

Married, single, children, brothers, sisters?
- I am single and I am an only child

What is your favorite skate race?
- Outdoor Nationals!

What activity do you like in the off season?
- I don't really have an off season but I enjoy spending time with loved ones as well as, being outdoors (love running!).

What is your favorite book or leisure activity?
- My favorite leisure activities are writing poetry, scrapbooking and listening to music!

What is your favorite vacation spot?
- Brazil because I have family there and there is great food, and there are beautiful sites to see.

What is your favorite kind of food?
- Any kind of potato!

How long have you been skating and competing?
- I have been skating for about 18 years, and competing in inline speed skating for about twelve years.

Most skaters, and coaches know me as "Nas". I am 23 years old and last spring I graduated from California State University East Bay with a B.A. in Psychology. I am currently working on a Masters degree in Kinesiology at the same University and may end up pursuing a Doctorate Degree in Sport Psychology!
