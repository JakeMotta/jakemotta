# Progression Survival 1.0.0-rc.7

Combined release with Journal0.8.0, independent Armor/Tools tracks,55 optional quests, Village Bounties, and personal rewards. Version labels are replaced by the package builder.

## Changes

- Material swords follow Tools stages; weapon/utility crafting belongs in Tools. Armor owns personal set crafting, combat and dungeon challenges.
- Combat requires the full matching personally crafted armor set. Weapon quotas start at Copper and total50% of each tier mob quota. A kill can satisfy one weapon category and its ordinary mob goal. No locked-tier overflow. No new trident, mace or spear requirements.
- Retuned material-aware goals and food progression. Wood pickaxe remains256 blocks. Later ore/food/fishing tasks require fresh work in their tier. Earlier unlocked tools can satisfy current-tier practice, avoiding golden-tool waste. Obsidian waits until Diamond access.
- Shield use requires crafting a shield and five skeleton kills in crafted Iron armor; previously completed Iron mastery preserves that access. Optional training rewards blocking20 arrows. Projectile credit uses the firing weapon.
- Moderate once-only task XP10–40points, plus rebalanced optional quest XP. Moved goals retain payout identity; completed historical tasks are not back-paid.
- Small personal supplies for intermediate tier completion; named enchanted capstones after completing Netherite. A full inventory defers the entire bundle; /claimrewards retries. Reward items never fabricate crafting proof. Previously completed tiers are historical rather than triggering a supply backlog.
- Village Bounties adds village boards with personal hunt/delivery contracts. Normal trades remain. Enchanted-gear bounty rewards are disabled; ordinary reward quantities are reduced. Completing10 bounties is optional and does not gate tiers.
- Improved Mobs retains personal health/damage scaling on Hard, with special AI/equipment, stealing/block breaking and speed/size changes disabled. Its world HUD stays hidden; inventory difficulty remains.
- Updated Prism instructions preserve preferences. This is an importable pack, not an automatic updater.

## Release contents

A versioned client .mrpack, matching reusable Windows server creator ZIP, pinned publisher downloads/hashes, our companion jar, rules/quests/settings, and friend instructions. No worlds, player data or administration secrets are included. Third-party jars download from publishers.

## Validation boundaries

Engine, Java, native Minecraft and packaging checks are recorded beside the release. Fresh survival pacing, real client rendering and multiplayer acceptance still need gameplay review. Hour estimates are design targets, not enforced timers or proven durations. Test fixtures are excluded. No website publishing is performed.

Rebuild with explicit version/journal jar, --scaling-only, the combined staged-server --overlay and its --extra-mods metadata; earlier builder defaults do not describe this release.
