2019–2022 · Background music for business

Ambii Business

Music for cafés, shops and restaurants. The owner picked stations, schedules and announcements in a dashboard, and the player in each store, a web page or a phone app, played them.

The servers behind it are long gone, so the real apps run here on a stand-in I wrote for them. You're signed in as Maya, who runs the seven stores of Bean & Beat Coffee Co. Change whatever you like: it's kept in your browser and goes nowhere else.

Things to try

  1. Play a store. Open the web player for Downtown Café and press play. Stick around for a couple of minutes and a welcome announcement plays over the music.
  2. Change what it plays. In the dashboard's Stations, open one and assign it to Downtown Café. The player switches when the song ends, just as it always did.
  3. Make an announcement. Under Announcements, type one, pick a voice and some background music, set it to play today two minutes from now and assign it to Downtown Café. Press skip in the web player so it picks the new one up. At that minute the music fades down and your browser reads it out.
  4. Steal the store. Pair the phone app with LBEAN7 while the web player is on it, and watch them hand over.
  5. Open stores from a spreadsheet. Under Locations, Upload CSV adds a whole list of stores at once. There's a sample file to try it with.
  6. Start your own business. Open the dashboard as a new customer, set up your first store and play it. Sign in again to get back to Maya.
  7. Drop to the free plan. Cancel the subscription under Upgrade, the crown in the dashboard's menu: ads play between songs and the extra stores switch off. Subscribe again there to bring them back.

What's different from the real thing

  • The musicThe licensed catalogue the stations drew from is gone. My own tracks and free music by other artists, credited below, play instead.
  • The voicesAnnouncements you make are read by your browser's voices instead of Google's. The ones that come with the demo are recordings.
  • PaymentsNothing is charged and no card details are asked for. Subscribing uses a sample card.
  • Sign-inYou're already signed in. Signing out and back in takes one click, no password.
