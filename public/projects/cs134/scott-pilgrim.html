SJSU CS 134 · 2017

Scott Pilgrim side-scroller

Class project for my game programming class · March 2017

My first game for the class: Scott runs, idles and punches across a scrolling level, and every punch throws a fireball. Ramona follows him around and jumps when he swings. The window was titled “I can't charge my phone and listen to music at the same time”.

It's written in Java straight on top of OpenGL (JOGL). There was no engine, so the sprite loading, animation, camera and collisions are mine too.

  • ←↑↓→ run
  • Z punch

Scott and Ramona's sprites come from Scott Pilgrim vs. the World: The Game (art by Paul Robertson), and the grass from Super Mario World (Nintendo). They were ripped for a class exercise; they aren't mine.

The clip was recorded in 2026 from the original 2017 build, with the music off.
