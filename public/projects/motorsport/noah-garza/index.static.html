About


Noah is an energetic sixth grader who is involved in the fastest growing sport in North America, Go-karting. He is 11 years old and is racing in the KPV1 and JR PRD classes.


When he is not racing, his favorite sport is basketball. He also likes to ride his skateboard and his bike. He also enjoys playing his Xbox.


He got bit by the racing bug at 5 years old and raced in his first race at Buttonwillow Raceway Park. Six years later he still has the bug bad and looks forward to every weekend at the track.
