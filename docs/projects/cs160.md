# CS160 (Face Me)

A 2016 class project by a five-person team (Austin Zurbuchen, Jacob Motta, Brendan Kao, Sidney Lai, Roben Angeles): upload a video and get it back with a face mesh drawn on every frame. Originally hosted at `jakemotta.com/CS160`.

## What the original did

A PHP site on Postgres. You signed up (`Register.php`) and logged in (`authenticate.php`), then uploaded a video on `Upload.php`, which ran a pipeline:
1. `postgres.c` read the video's size, fps and frame count with ffprobe, saved a row, and split the video into PNG frames with ffmpeg.
2. `datapoints_script` ran OpenFace `FaceLandmarkImg` on the frames, writing 68 landmarks per frame to `<id>.<frame>_det_0.pts`.
3. `delaunay_script` ran `delaunay.cpp` on each frame. It used OpenCV's Subdiv2D to draw the Delaunay mesh in blue and the points in red, then ffmpeg joined the frames into `OUTPUT.webm`.

`Videos.php` listed your videos and played the output in a lightbox.

## Preview

The app had a real back end, so it isn't converted. The static pages sit beside the restored originals:
- **`index.html`** is the hosted login page, with the two output videos and a live face plot, so visitors see the result without signing up.
- **The flow:** `SignUp.html`, `Register.html`, `Upload.html` and `Videos.html` (My Videos), plus `Output.html?v=out1|out2|landmarks|<upload id>`, a result page standing in for the lightbox, and `logout.html`.
- **`js/faceme.js` fakes the back end.** Any username and password logs in, and only the username is kept, in sessionStorage. "Upload" never reads or sends the file: it plays the pipeline's steps, then adds a sample entry to localStorage. My Videos starts with the two real outputs and the landmark animation. Nothing leaves the browser.
- **`js/faceplot.js`** stands in for `delaunay.cpp`. It triangulates each frame of `js/landmarks.js` (Bowyer–Watson) and draws it in the same colors at 30 fps, with play/pause, a frame scrubber and mesh/points toggles.
- **`js/landmarks.js`** is generated from the `.pts` files: 374 detected frames out of 430. A frame with no face holds the previous one.
- **`extras.html`**, linked as "Extras" in the front page's footer, shows the five concept mockups (dated December 2016 by Fireworks; click one to enlarge it in a `<dialog>`) and both output videos.
  - **Files:** it uses the mockups in `extras/` and `concept/` and the videos in `videos/` in place, so nothing is duplicated.
  - **Sound:** the videos have no sound track, so they autoplay muted on a loop like the Output page, and the page doesn't pause the site's music.

The `.pts` points came from a small, low-resolution test video whose frames aren't in the history, so the animation draws on a plain background. `videos/out1.webm` and `out2.webm` are the original pipeline's own output, with the mesh drawn over Jake's face.
