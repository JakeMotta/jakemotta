Ambii

The startup I worked on from 2017 to 2021: a music app for listening together, where everyone in the room gets a say in what plays next. Here it is in pieces: pitch day, the first wireframes, the beta, its websites, two videos, and the designs along the way.

Wireframes

Before the apps, the web app got drawn out screen by screen: finding a party, starting one, the party itself, adding songs. The second round is clickable, so go ahead and poke at it.

2018 – 2019

The websites

The landing page went up in 2018. Two more sites followed: one started on a template and never finished, and the 2019 site that went with the apps.

The first ambii.io landing page: socialize, influence, discover.

A new site begun on a Colorlib template. The top half is Ambii's; further down it's still the template's placeholder copy. Saved in February 2019.
